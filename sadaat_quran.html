About the Academy

A calm, structured way to learn the Qur'an online

Sadaat Quran Academy was founded to bring authentic, Tajweed-correct Qur'an education into every home — without long commutes or rigid madrassa timings. Every student is matched with a qualified teacher and follows a clear, trackable learning path, from the first Arabic letter to complete memorization.

Our Mission

To make authentic Qur'an education accessible to every home, taught with sincerity and care.

Our Vision

To be a trusted name in online Qur'an learning — known for character, not just marketing.

FAQ

Common questions

Is the free trial class really free?

Yes. You can book one trial class with no payment and no obligation to continue, so you can meet the teacher first.

Can we choose a male or female teacher?

Yes — let us know your preference when you enroll and we will match you accordingly.

What age groups do you teach?

We teach children from age 5 and above, as well as adult learners of any age or starting level.

What do we need for online classes?

A phone, tablet or laptop with a camera, and a stable internet connection — no special software required.

How is payment handled?

Fees are billed monthly with several payment options; full details are shared after your trial class.

Can classes be rescheduled?

Yes, with reasonable advance notice through WhatsApp, classes can be moved without penalty.
